Offline PDF table extraction for Mac.

PDFTrail converts born-digital and scanned PDFs to Excel, Word, Markdown, or plain text on your Mac. Table extraction is built for financial analysts: annual reports in, clean spreadsheets out. Everything runs offline and on-device — see the privacy policy.

Free tier & unlock
  • Free: unlimited conversions, full on-screen table preview, on-device OCR.
  • Free exports: unlimited plain text, one full Markdown export, one full Word export, and Excel exports of the first 15 rows of each table.
  • The bundled sample annual report exports fully in every format.
  • One in-app purchase, "Unlock Full Version", removes every limit. One payment. No subscription.
